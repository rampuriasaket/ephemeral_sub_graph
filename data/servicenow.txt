INCIDENT: INC1042
TITLE: Users unable to log in — auth-service returning 401 on valid credentials
STATUS: Resolved
PRIORITY: P1 - Critical
ASSIGNED TO: Priya Nair (Platform Reliability)
CREATED: 2026-08-01 03:12 UTC
RESOLVED: 2026-08-01 05:47 UTC
RELATED WORK: PROJ-201
--------------------------------------------------------------------
DESCRIPTION:
Starting around 03:00 UTC, customer support began receiving a spike in
tickets from users reporting they could not log in despite entering
correct credentials. The authentication service (auth-service) was
returning HTTP 401 for a large percentage of login attempts. Initial
triage confirmed this was not a customer-side issue — internal test
accounts were also failing. Traffic to auth-service was otherwise
healthy, ruling out a capacity issue.

RESOLUTION NOTES:
Root cause was traced to an expired JWT signing key that had not been
rotated ahead of its expiry window. Once the key was rotated, login
success rates returned to baseline within minutes. A follow-up story
was opened (PROJ-201) to add automated alerting before key expiry.
Engineering confirmed the fix via a hotfix pull request. A postmortem
document has been drafted by the Platform team.
